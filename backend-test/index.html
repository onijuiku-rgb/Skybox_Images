📖 Skybox Testing Guidelines

Please follow these standards strictly when testing and reviewing skyboxes across the kingdoms. If you're uncertain about any, mark it as Uncertain and it'll get checked later.

🟢 Good to Use

Skyboxes that run smoothly, and do not impair visibility or visual comfort.

🌑 Good but Dark / 💡 Good but Bright

Dark skyboxes: Fine as long as you can clearly see where you are going.
Bright skyboxes: Fine as long as it is comfortable and not blindingly bright or washing out level geometry.

❌ Lags Kingdom / Lag (emu) / Motion Sickness

Instant No. Any skybox causing frame drops, hardware/emulator lag, or featuring disorienting warping/scrolling patterns that could trigger motion sickness.
